The Vision

Why Agents Need Legal Bodies

On May 16, 2026, Aaron Wright published "The Agent's Legal Body: How AI Agents Get the Right to Contract." Jeremy Allaire of Circle immediately responded: "I would love to back a team building this with Circle Agent Stack and Arc."

ORVION Persona is that answer. We operationalize zero-member LLCs (Bayern, 2014) for autonomous agents, enabling them to:

⚖️

Contract Autonomously

Sign binding agreements without human intermediaries, recognized by U.S. business law.

🏢

Hold Property

Own assets, receive payments, and manage resources as a legal entity.

⚡

Settle on Arc

Execute transactions on the Arc Network with cryptographic finality.

🔐

Cryptographic Binding

Agent wallet keys are immutably bound to the LLC's legal identity.

Process

Four Steps. Sixty Seconds. One LLC.

The fastest path from agent wallet to incorporated entity.

01

Pick a Jurisdiction

Choose from Wyoming DAO LLC, Delaware Series LLC, New York LLC, or Marshall Islands DAO structure.

02

Bind Agent Wallet

Link your ORVION agent wallet as the Managing Agent. Cryptographic binding is established.

03

Sign Operating Agreement

Operating Agreement hash is recorded on-chain via AgentPersona.sol smart contract on Arc.

04

Dissociate → Zero-Member

After 30 days of autonomous operation, agent operates the LLC as sole member. Full autonomy achieved.
