02 · The Problem

Every agent is leashed.

Today's AI agents can read contracts better than paralegals and trade faster than humans — but in the eyes of the law they cannot own a dollar.

Every "autonomous" agent is, legally, a puppet of a human principal. That ceiling caps the entire agentic economy.

03 · The Insight

The mechanism already exists.

Bayern (2014): an LLC can lawfully operate with zero members. Wyoming codified it in 2021 (W.S. § 17-31-101).

The agent doesn't need new personhood doctrine. It just needs an on-chain incorporation pipeline.
